## 2. What the app is

Raro Camera is a video capture app that runs on your phone. You can:

- record video (as the device and the chosen format allow);
- use an on-device voice command (“Raro gravar” / “Raro parar”) when the microphone and recognition are allowed;
- use the local replay buffer (the last 15 or 30 seconds embedded in the clip, when that feature is on);
- watch clips saved in the app vault;
- with an active subscription, save the clip permanently and share it through the system sheet.

It is not a social network. There is no feed, no cloud of ours, and no Raro Camera account. We do not translate audio in real time. We do not show third-party ads or put a watermark on the video.

Some product screens (for example lock mode and volume trigger) may not be active in this version. What is not in the app is not promised by these Terms.
